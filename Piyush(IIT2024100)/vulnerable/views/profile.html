Why This App Is Vulnerable (POST-based CSRF)

  • No Anti-CSRF Token: POST /account/update does not require a secret token.
  • No Origin / Referer Check: Requests from http://localhost:4000 are accepted.
  • Weak Session Cookie: sid_vuln has no SameSite=Strict attribute.
